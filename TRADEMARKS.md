# Trademark Policy

"AeroFlex" and the AeroFlex logo are trademarks of Delta Robotics Inc.
The Apache-2.0 license covers the content of this repository; it does not
grant trademark rights (see section 6 of the [LICENSE](LICENSE)).

The goal of this policy is simple: anyone should be able to build, sell, and
talk about products that work with the AeroFlex Standard, while "AeroFlex"
itself keeps meaning something specific and trustworthy.

## You may, without asking

- State compatibility descriptively: "compatible with the AeroFlex Standard",
  "works with AeroFlex muscles", "built for the AeroFlex ecosystem".
- Use the name in articles, papers, videos, and tutorials about the standard.
- Keep the name in unmodified copies of these docs, as the license requires.

## Please ask first (mark@deltaroboticsinc.com)

- Naming a product, company, or domain with "AeroFlex" in it
  (for example "AeroFlex Pro Gripper" or "aeroflex-store.com").
- Using the AeroFlex name or logo in a way that implies your product is made,
  certified, or endorsed by Delta Robotics.
- Using the logo on packaging or marketing.

## Never

- Rebrand modified versions of the standard itself as "AeroFlex". If you fork
  and change the spec, give your fork its own name and note that it derives
  from the AeroFlex Standard.

A lightweight compatibility program ("AeroFlex Compatible" marks with basic
conformance checks) is planned; until then, descriptive statements as above
are the way to signal compatibility.
